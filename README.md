Drinking Game Tournament Tool

Meant for 2-4 players. Players are assigned teams and then take turns drafting games for a best of 3, 5 or 7 series. Free for all modes are first to a certain amount of wins.

The tournament begins with the second drafting team picking a game to play first. Gameplay consists of a round of whatever game is chosen. 
Additional rules for each game indicate sips that can be given to opponents when doing something positive or for the player who caused something negative. 
Totals are calculated for stats for now, other functionality might come later. 
Loser of a round takes a shot, but has choice of the next game.

This continues until the win condition is met. Additional forfeit for the loser might be added.
